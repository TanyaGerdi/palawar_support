About Palawar

Palawar is an agricultural B2B and B2C digital coordination platform operating across the Kurdistan Region of Iraq. The platform connects registered poultry retail shops, authorized distribution agents, and commercial farm producers to streamline daily market price discovery and livestock logistics.
