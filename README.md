# Palawar (پەلەوەڕ) - Official Support & Legal Center

Welcome to the official legal compliance, support, and documentation portal for the **Palawar** mobile application on iOS.

Palawar is a multi-service B2B & B2C platform operating in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq, connecting poultry merchants, authorized service agents, legal advisors, and sworn translators.
