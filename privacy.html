Privacy Policy (سیاسەتی پاراستنی نهێنی)

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026 | Palawar Application

This Privacy Policy explains how Palawar ("we", "our", or "us") collects, utilizes, safeguards, and deletes personal information when you use the Palawar mobile application and related agricultural commerce services in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq.

1. Information We Collect (Apple App Privacy Compliance)

In accordance with Apple App Store Privacy Disclosures, the Palawar app collects the following specific data categories to provide core agricultural livestock and trade logistics features:

  • Contact Information:
    • Name & Business Name: Used to identify poultry retail shops, authorized distribution agents, and commercial farm producers within the directory.
    • Email Address: Used for account registration, security alerts, and customer support.
    • Phone Number: Used for verification, operational order logistics, and driver delivery dispatches.
  • User-Generated Content & Communications:
    • In-App Messages: Direct 1-to-1 operational inquiries regarding poultry batches, order specifications, and delivery scheduling.
    • Photos / Document Attachments: Photos of live poultry batches, delivery receipts, or veterinary inspection notes uploaded voluntarily by sellers.
  • Identifiers:
    • User ID: A unique internal identifier used to associate your account with your trade portal preferences.
    • Device Token: Used solely to deliver push notifications regarding order confirmations and message alerts via Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) and Pusher Beams.
  • Coarse Location Data:
    • Used strictly on an opt-in basis to calculate distance between retail shops and nearby authorized distribution agents or farms to coordinate regional livestock shipments. Location is never tracked in the background.
  • Audio Data (Real-Time VoIP):
    • Real-time peer-to-peer audio and video consultations between poultry farm operators and authorized veterinary or logistics agents powered by Agora RTC. Audio streams are processed in real-time and are NEVER recorded or stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate commercial operations:

  • Facilitating livestock order discovery between registered poultry shops and verified suppliers.
  • Enabling direct communication between buyers and logistics agents.
  • Authenticating user sessions and safeguarding accounts against unauthorized access.
  • Delivering push notifications regarding active order dispatches.

3. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

To deliver robust functionality, Palawar interacts with vetted industry-standard service providers:

  • Agora RTC: Powers live voice and video consultations between traders. Audio/video streams are ephemeral and unrecorded.
  • Pusher Channels & Beams: Powers real-time message synchronization and push notification dispatches.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): Delivers background APNs notifications to iOS devices.
  • Apple Sign In: Secure, privacy-preserving authentication provided by Apple Inc.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1)

We believe in complete user ownership of personal data. You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time:

Instant In-App Deletion:

Open Palawar → Tap Profile → Scroll down and tap Delete Account (سڕینەوەی ئەژمێر) → Confirm. Your user account, active sessions, and identifying tokens are immediately and permanently removed from our production database.

If you have uninstalled the app and wish to delete your data, you can submit an email request to support@palawar.com or visit our Account Deletion Page. Requests are completed within 24 hours.

5. User Safety & Content Moderation (Guideline 1.2)

Palawar maintains a zero-tolerance policy for abusive users or objectionable content. Every user can:

  • Report Content: Long-press any chat message or tap the menu in any profile to submit an immediate report to our safety moderation team. Reports are reviewed and actioned within 24 hours.
  • Block Users: Instantly block any abusive user from the chat options menu. Blocked users can no longer message or call you.

6. Security and Encryption

All network communications between the Palawar client application and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Sensitive credentials and authorization tokens on the device are stored within the secure Apple iOS Keychain.
