Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I switch between the Poultry Shop, Legal, and Translation portals?
From the main screen, tap the side navigation menu (or the portal switcher button in the top header) and choose "Change Portal" (گۆڕینی پۆڕتاڵ) to access Poultry Market, Legal Advisory, or Sworn Translation services.
2. Can I browse the poultry market as a Guest?
Yes. Guests can browse daily market prices, view service directories, and read public announcements. However, initiating live chats, booking consultations, managing wallet balances, and viewing order history require signing in to protect user security and data integrity.
3. How are physical live poultry orders fulfilled?
Palawar connects authorized poultry suppliers and retail butchers. Orders for live poultry are delivered via local ground logistics and settled outside the app (cash on delivery or verified commercial bank transfer) in compliance with Apple Guideline 3.1.5(a) for physical goods.
4. How do I report abusive content or block another user?
In compliance with Apple Guideline 1.2 (User-Generated Content), you can report any inappropriate message by long-pressing the message or tapping the options menu inside any active chat and selecting "Report Message". You can also immediately block any user by selecting "Block User". Blocked users are immediately hidden and cannot contact you.
5. How do I delete my Palawar account and personal data?
You can delete your account anytime directly within the app: Navigate to Drawer → Settings / Profile → Delete Account. Alternatively, visit our Account Deletion Request Page to submit an immediate data erasure request.
