# Palawar Account & Data Deletion

**Compliance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1**

---

## How to Delete Your Account

### In-App Deletion (Immediate)
1. Open the Palawar application.
2. Tap the drawer navigation menu icon at the top corner.
3. Tap your Profile / Name to open account settings.
4. Scroll to the bottom and select **"Delete Account" (سڕینەوەی هەژمار)**.
5. Confirm deletion in the confirmation dialog.
6. Your account, authentication tokens, chat records, and device notification registrations will be permanently deleted immediately.

### Web / Email Deletion Request
If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, email our support desk:
- **Email:** [support@palawar.com](mailto:support@palawar.com)
- **Subject:** Palawar Account Deletion Request
- **Include:** Registered phone number and full name.

All requests are processed and matching server records purged within 24 to 48 hours.
