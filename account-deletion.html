Account & Personal Data Deletion Request

Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1 (Data Retention and Erasure Compliance)

Palawar respects your right to privacy and full control over your personal information. You can permanently delete your Palawar account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1: In-App Self-Service Deletion (Immediate)

The fastest way to permanently erase your account is directly inside the Palawar iOS application:

1

Open the Side Navigation Drawer

From the home screen of any portal, tap your profile avatar or the menu icon at the top corner.

2

Select Profile / Account Settings

Tap on your name/profile card to access account details.

3

Tap "Delete Account" (سڕینەوەی هەژمار)

Scroll to the bottom and select "Delete Account". A confirmation modal will appear.

4

Confirm Permanent Deletion

Confirm the prompt. The app immediately purges your active authentication session, invalidates push tokens on Pusher Beams, deletes local caches, and calls the API deletion endpoint.

Option 2: Online Deletion Request (Web-based)

If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can request immediate manual deletion by contacting our privacy compliance team:

Submit an Erasure Request via Email

Please send an email from or referencing the phone number / email associated with your Palawar account. Our administrators process and purge all matching records within 24 to 48 hours.

Send Deletion Request Email

What Data is Deleted?

Upon account deletion, the following data is permanently erased from active production databases:

  • Profile Information: Full name, phone number, email address, and profile photo.
  • Messaging & Chats: All live chat messages, voice notes, and media attachments sent by you.
  • Device Identifiers: Push notification device tokens, Pusher Beams device interests, and authentication tokens.
  • Location Data: Any stored shop address or coarse coordinate logs.

Note: Completed physical delivery tax receipts or commercial settlement records required by local trade laws are archived for statutory retention periods and cannot be used for any other purpose.
