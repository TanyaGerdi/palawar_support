# Palawar Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 6, 2026  
**Developer:** SD SOFT / Twana Ibrahimi  
**Platform:** iOS (App Store)

---

## 1. Introduction
Palawar ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Palawar multi-service platform connecting poultry merchants, legal specialists, and certified translators. This Privacy Policy details the types of data collected, its purpose, storage, and user rights in full accordance with the **Apple App Store Review Guidelines**.

## 2. Data Types Collected & App Store Privacy Declarations
In full alignment with our App Store Connect App Privacy disclosures, Palawar collects the following categories of data linked to user identity:

| Data Type | Purpose | Linked to Identity? |
|---|---|---|
| **Contact Information** (Name, Phone Number, Email) | App Functionality & Authentication | Yes |
| **User Content** (Chat Messages, Audio Notes, Photos) | Direct messaging and service consultation | Yes |
| **Identifiers** (User ID, Device Token) | Push notifications & multi-session support | Yes |
| **Location** (Coarse Location / City) | Regional market price routing and logistics | Yes |
| **Audio Data** (Microphone voice notes) | User-to-agent voice messaging during active consultation | Yes |

We **do not** collect any financial data (banking passwords or credit card numbers), nor do we sell user data or engage in cross-app third-party advertising tracking.

## 3. How We Use Information
- Authenticate registered merchants, lawyers, translators, and customers.
- Facilitate real-time communications through Pusher Channels & Agora RTC.
- Send transactional order updates and appointment reminders via Pusher Beams.
- Ensure community safety and enforce our zero-tolerance policy against abusive content.

## 4. User-Generated Content & Privacy Protections
User chats are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3. Users have full control over their chat data and can report abuse or block any contact at any time.

## 5. Account Deletion & Right to Erasure
Users may permanently delete their account and associated data directly in the app (Drawer -> Profile -> Delete Account) or online at [Account Deletion](account-deletion.html). All profile details, tokens, and messages are erased upon confirmation.
